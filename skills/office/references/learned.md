## Lifecycle

1. `Capture` writes `.opencode/office/.capture/<hash>.json` per `officecli` invoke (same shape as `tests/isolated-workspace/.capture/`).
2. On `accept` with `verify-l3 PASS`, `skills/office` proposes a record (proposal is a draft, not auto-written).
3. You review `learned.json` diff, then `accept` the proposal — this writes `.opencode/office/learned/learned.json`, regenerates this `learned.md` view.
4. Next session, skill replays `records` matching `templateHash`/`structureHash` to skip Verify Loop (`set/add → query`) — 2–3 min instead of 30 min.

## Promotion

Per-project `.opencode/office/learned/learned.json` is local. When a pattern proves generic (verified across 2+ dossiers), manually copy its `fix`/`structure` to `skills/office/references/learned.md` and propose an `ADR` for `src/` In-place Fix. This follows `Single write path`.
